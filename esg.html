ESG Commitment

Driving a Sustainable Future in Malaysia

At Norumoto, we believe that the transition to clean mobility must be accessible, responsible, and transparent.

Environmental

(E)

Zero Emissions, Maximum Impact

Every Norumoto S90 on the road represents a direct reduction in Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions compared to traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) motorcycles.

  • ✓ Zero tailpipe emissions.
  • ✓ Responsible battery lifecycle management and recycling initiatives.
  • ✓ Energy-efficient manufacturing processes.

Social

(S)

Empowering the Community

Clean mobility should not be a luxury. We are committed to empowering the B40 community and daily commuters with transport that drastically reduces their cost of living.

  • ✓ Affordable pricing structures and Shariah-compliant financing.
  • ✓ Strict adherence to safety standards (JPJ VTA compliance).
  • ✓ Supporting local economies and gig workers.

Governance

(G)

Transparent & Accountable

We operate with the highest standards of business integrity. Our future roadmap includes transparent carbon credit tracking and robust ESG compliance reporting for our corporate and B2B fleet partners.

The more you ride, the more you save.

(And the more the environment benefits).
